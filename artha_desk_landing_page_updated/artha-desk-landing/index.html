Can I use Artha-Desk without GST registration?

Yes. The business profile can be configured for an unregistered business. The system should not show GST as a separately collected tax or present the document as a GST tax invoice.

Does it support composition taxpayers?

The planned workflow includes composition-scheme billing and bill-of-supply handling. The business configuration and restrictions must be validated before production use.

Can I print thermal receipts?

The product is planned to support common 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt layouts, subject to printer and browser compatibility testing.

Can my staff use it in Odia?

The product direction includes an English and Odia interface, including bilingual product names where configured.
